What's Not Included

Direct Fees (Not Managed by Havn Stays)

Some costs are directly related to the property and its operation. These fees are not part of our 20% commission:

  • Extraordinary Cleaning Fees - Usually charged directly to traveler as security deposit
  • Major Work and Renovations - Réparations structurelles, changements importants : vous gérez ou nous vous recommandons nos partenaires
  • Furniture and Decoration - Purchases, replacement, or furniture upgrades remain your responsibility

Ces coûts sont des dépenses d'exploitation directes de la propriété, pas des frais de management. Havn Stays facture uniquement la commission de gestion.

Questions About Our Pricing

Why 20% and not less?

+

Our 20% covers a complete suite of services: professional photography, premium listings, dynamic pricing, multilingual 24/7 support, cleaning, maintenance, legal compliance, and reporting. Compared to traditional agencies (25-35%) plus hidden fees, you actually save with Havn Stays while benefiting from world-class management.

How is the commission calculated?

+

The 20% commission is calculated on the amount received per stay (after platform payment). For example: a 1,000 € stay generates 200 € in Havn Stays fees, and you receive 800 € net. No hidden fees, no surcharges, no ambiguity. It's clear and transparent each month in your dashboard.

Y a-t-il des frais supplémentaires ?

+

None. The only costs beyond our 20% are direct property expenses: extraordinary cleaning fees (charged to travelers), major renovations, new furniture. These are operational costs, not management fees. Havn Stays only charges the agreed commission.

Can I negotiate the rate?

+

For owners with multiple villas or long-term commitments (2-3 years), we discuss special conditions. But our 20% rate remains competitive given the quality provided. Contact us to explore a personalized partnership.

When do I receive my revenue?

+

Revenue is paid monthly, usually between the 25th-28th of the following month. Par exemple : les séjours de mars sont payés fin avril. Vous pouvez suivre chaque transaction en direct dans votre dashboard Havn Stays mis à jour quotidiennement.
