Our Coverage Areas

Seven premium areas,
seven strategies.

Havn Stays operates across Morocco's premium villa markets. From Marrakech's iconic Palmeraie each area has its own characteristics, clientele, and revenue potential. Discover yours.

La Palmeraie
The Prestige

The most iconic area of Marrakech. High-end villas with landscaped gardens, private pools, and access to the region's finest amenities. La Palmeraie is synonymous with luxury and attracts an international high-net-worth clientele.

Guest profile
International clientele, affluent families, groups, and private events
Indicative rate
4,000 — 15,000 MAD/night depending on amenities
Ideal for
4+ bedroom villas with pool and premium services
Route de l'Ourika
Authentic Charm

Contemporary villas with panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains. Spacious properties with generous gardens and proximity to nature. This area appeals to couples and families who appreciate authenticity and mountain landscapes. Excellent value for money.

Guest profile
Couples, families, nature lovers, and hiking enthusiasts
Indicative rate
2,500 — 8,500 MAD/night for attractive offerings
Ideal for
3-5 bedroom villas with terraces and views
Route d'Amizmiz
The Exclusivity

Exceptional estates on large grounds, away from the hustle. Complete tranquility, guaranteed privacy, and dreamlike settings. Route d'Amizmiz attracts ultra-premium clients seeking discretion and absolute luxury for private events and exclusive retreats.

Guest profile
Ultra-premium clientele, exclusive events, celebrities, intimate stays
Indicative rate
5,000 — 25,000+ MAD/night for prestige estates
Ideal for
Large estates 5+ bedrooms with event spaces
Route de Fès
Emerging Market

Dynamic emerging area with modern residential projects. Contemporary villas with high-end finishes. Excellent opportunity for investors with better mid-term ROI potential. Growing market.

Guest profile
Modern travelers, families, couples, business professionals
Indicative rate
2,800 — 9,500 MAD/night for new villas
Ideal for
Modern 3-4 bedroom villas with contemporary design
Amelkis & Golf
The Lifestyle

High-end residential area in close proximity to the Royal Amelkis Golf Club. Neighborhood favored by golf enthusiasts, corporate retreats, and sports travelers. Premium infrastructure and privileged access to facilities. Distinct lifestyle positioning.

Guest profile
Golfers, corporate groups, sports retreats, corporate events
Indicative rate
3,500 — 12,000 MAD/night for golf-access properties
Ideal for
3-5 bedroom villas near golf
Route du Barrage
Grand Estates

Properties with large grounds, true spaciousness, and authentic atmosphere. Ideal for events, extended retreats, and groups. Generous villas offering versatility and event potential. Excellent for extended stays.

Guest profile
Private events, extended stays, groups, large families
Indicative rate
3,000 — 11,000 MAD/night for large spaces
Ideal for
Grand estates 4-6+ bedrooms with outdoor spaces
Targa & Agdal
Close to City Center

High-end residential neighborhoods close to the Medina and main attractions. Maximum accessibility for short stays and urban travelers. Ideal for those seeking luxury without isolation. Strategic locations.

Guest profile
Urban travelers, short stays, explorers, couples
Indicative rate
2,200 — 8,000 MAD/night depending on city proximity
Ideal for
2-4 bedroom villas well-located, accessible
