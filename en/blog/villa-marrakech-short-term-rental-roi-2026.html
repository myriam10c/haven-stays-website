How much does a villa in Marrakech really earn on short-term rental in 2026?

3 real cases, actual numbers and a calculation method to estimate your Marrakech villa's profitability on Airbnb and Booking in 2026.

"In 2026, how much will it actually earn me?"

The honest answer: it depends — on neighborhood, capacity, listing quality, and crucially, on who runs the operation day-to-day. So instead of theory, here are 3 real cases from the HAVN Stays portfolio over 2025 and early 2026.

How to actually do the math

Short-term rental revenue is straightforward:

Gross revenue = average daily rate (ADR) × nights sold Net revenue = gross – platform fees – operating costs – management – tax

Where:

  • ADR: average price per night across all seasons.
  • Occupancy rate: nights sold / 365.
  • Platform fees: 3 % host-side on Airbnb, 15-18 % host-side on Booking.
  • Operating costs: hotel-grade housekeeping, linen, laundry, consumables, pool, garden, utilities, internet, council tax, insurance.
  • Professional management: 18 to 25 % of gross, depending on scope.
  • Tax: Moroccan rental income tax (flat or actual), VAT 10 % above thresholds.

💡 Rule of thumb: without professional management, expect 35 to 45 % net margin. With premium concierge, net margin drops to 25-35 %, but gross revenue rises 30 to 50 % thanks to revenue management. Owners pocket more.

What the numbers actually say

  • A well-run 3-bedroom villa: 7 to 9 % gross / 5 to 6 % net.
  • A 4-bedroom in Palmeraie: 9 to 12 % gross / 6 to 7 % net.
  • A premium 5-bedroom: 12 to 16 % gross / 7 to 8 % net (more with add-on services).

Three levers move profitability from "okay" to "great":

  1. Dynamic revenue management — pricing per night (+30 to 50 % gross uplift observed).
  2. Pro photos + optimized listing — 2x conversion (+15 to 25 % occupancy).
  3. Guest scores > 4.9 — unlocks Airbnb "Guest Favorite" and Booking premium placement.

What a serious projection must include

When you receive a revenue forecast, check that it covers:

  • ✅ A monthly ADR (Marrakech is highly seasonal — peaks Oct-Dec and Feb-Apr).
  • ✅ Realistic occupancy (60-75 % avg, not 90 %).
  • ✅ All operating costs (housekeeping, linen, pool, garden, utilities).
  • ✅ The management commission AND what's in / out of scope.
  • ✅ Moroccan taxation (rental income tax, VAT, council tax, tourist tax).
  • ✅ An annual CapEx reserve (3-5 % of gross for refresh).

A projection that promises 15 % net without itemized costs is a red flag.

FAQ — Marrakech villa rental ROI 2026

❓ What is the average occupancy of a Marrakech villa? 60 to 75 % annually, with peaks at 90 % October-December and February-April. Strong seasonality but the shoulder seasons are filling in thanks to wellness tourism, golf and corporate groups.

❓ Average ADR for a Marrakech villa in 2026? Standard 3-bed villa: 1,500 – 2,500 MAD. Palmeraie/Hivernage 4-bed: 3,000 – 5,000 MAD. Premium 5-bed: 4,500 – 8,000 MAD (peaks at 12,000 MAD during holidays and 2030 World Cup).

❓ Doesn't management fee eat into my returns? On the contrary. A pro concierge takes 18-25 % commission but lifts gross revenue 30-50 %, handles compliance, and saves you considerable time. Net is almost always higher than self-managing.

❓ What about non-residents (French, Belgian, Swiss owners)? Income is taxable in Morocco (rental income tax). Under tax treaties, it is then declared in your home country with double-taxation relief. Our Sérénité offer handles all tax filings.
