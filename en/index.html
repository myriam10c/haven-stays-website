Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What does the 20% management fee include?
All operational services: listing creation, pricing optimization, multi-platform distribution, guest communication, check-in/check-out, cleaning coordination, maintenance management, and monthly reporting. No hidden fees.
How much more can I earn compared to an annual lease?
Most villa owners see 30-40% additional revenue from short-term rentals, depending on location and season. We provide a free estimate before any commitment.
Can I use my villa for personal use?
Absolutely. Your villa, your dates. Simply let us know your preferred periods and we'll block the calendar accordingly.
How long does it take to get started?
From signing to first booking, expect 5-10 days. This includes villa inspection, professional photography, listing creation, and pricing setup.
What types of properties do you manage?
We exclusively manage villas and riads. No apartments. This specialization allows us to provide expertise and care that general agencies cannot match.
What areas do you cover?
We manage villas in Marrakech (all major neighborhoods: Palmeraie, Route de l'Ourika, Route d'Amizmiz, Amelkis, Route de Fès, Targa, Agdal, and more), Taghazout Bay, and Agadir. Contact us to discuss your property.
Who pays for cleaning?
Cleaning is a direct operational cost. Depending on your setup, it can be charged to guests (cleaning fee on the platform) or deducted from rental revenue. We advise on the most competitive approach.
