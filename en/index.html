PROTOCOL 05 · FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you want to know
before handing us your keys.

No fine print. No gray areas. Real answers to real owner questions.

A single, fixed commission on collected revenue, no markup on external services (pool maintenance, gardening, cleaning). You only pay commission on revenue actually generated.

Onboarding fees may apply depending on the property (setup, photo shoot, additional equipment, welcome book). They are quoted precisely during the free audit, based on your villa.

Two overlapping protections: (1) platform insurance (AirCover Airbnb, Booking Partners) covering guest-caused damage; (2) your own home insurance for exceptional events.

In practice, immediately after check-out, our team inspects the villa on-site and photographs every room. If damage is found, you are notified instantly via the owner portal — with photo evidence — and we handle the reimbursement procedure end-to-end.

On-site inspection after each check-out, with time-stamped photo report of every room archived in your owner portal. All access points (Wi-Fi, alarm, safe, gate codes) are centralized internally and never shared in plaintext.

Every physical key is tracked via a signed handover system. Permanent audit log: you see in your portal who entered, when, why.

Yes, with no penalty and a 30-day notice. You take back your keys, access, booking history, contacts. Your villa stays your villa.

If a booking is already confirmed during the notice period, we honor it through check-out, then we hand you a complete dossier.

You receive your rental revenue in your own account, net of our commission. We provide a detailed annual tax summary, ready for your accountant, compliant with Moroccan rental income tax requirements and, if applicable, with your country-of-residence taxation.

For non-residents, we work with a partner accounting firm in Marrakech that can handle the entire declaration if you wish.

Seven days from contract to first guest, detailed in the "Onboarding" section above. Audit, contract, shoot, digital setup, pricing, welcome book, go-live — every step documented.
