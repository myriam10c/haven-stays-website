Amelkis & Golf

Why Amelkis & Golf is a solid rental asset

Amelkis and the adjoining golf residences (Royal Palm, Samanah, Al Maaden) form a premium cluster south-east of Marrakech. 10–15 minutes from the centre, these residences offer a unique mix: 24/7 security, golf courses, common areas, and high-end villas. An area particularly suited to consistent rental operation.

Area profile
Premium golf villas and resort residences

Clientele is high-end and mixed: European golfers on short breaks (3–5 nights), holiday families, Gulf expats. The secure nature of residences reassures non-resident owners — concierge operates with clean, professional conditions.

Villas have 3 to 6 bedrooms, with pool, course views, and club access. Average nightly rates range from 3,000 to 9,000 MAD. High golf season (October–April) generates strong occupancy; golf is also a powerful direct booking driver (packages).
