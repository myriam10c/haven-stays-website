Detailed Funnel Stages:

Stage 1: Visitor → Lead (Days 0-3)

Touchpoint: Website estimation form (or lead magnet download)

CTA: "Get Free Rental Estimate in 5 Minutes" or "Download: Villa Owner's Checklist"

Form Fields: Name, email, phone, villa location, current status, estimated value

Follow-up: Automated email (same day) + WhatsApp message with next steps

Target Conversion: 8-12% of website visitors

Stage 2: Lead → Call Booked (Days 1-7)

Touchpoint: Phone call (Hillal or team member) to discuss property + needs

CTA: "Schedule a 20-minute consultation call"

Call Script: Qualify prospect (villa type, status, pain points), share initial insights, address objections

Follow-up: Calendar invite sent after call; WhatsApp reminder 24h before

Target Conversion: 50% of leads book call

Stage 3: Call → Villa Visit (Days 7-21)

Touchpoint: In-person or video tour of villa (Havn team assesses property)

CTA: "Let's walk through your property together"

Visit Agenda: Property assessment, occupancy analysis, pricing review, guest experience gaps, renovation opportunities

Deliverable: Written "Property Assessment Report" (PDF) with recommendations + projected ROI

Follow-up: Email assessment report + schedule follow-up call

Target Conversion: 70% of calls convert to visit

Stage 4: Villa Visit → Contract Signed (Days 21-45)

Touchpoint: Management proposal + contract presentation

CTA: "Let's start managing your villa" + sign agreement

Proposal Includes: Service scope, pricing structure (20% commission), estimated ROI, timeline, payment terms

Objection Handling: Price comparison, performance guarantees, trial period (optional first 1-2 months at 15%)

Follow-up: Signed contract + onboarding checklist sent

Target Conversion: 60% of visits convert to signed contract

Stage 5: Signed → Active Management (Days 45-60)

Touchpoint: Onboarding week with owner + property listing launches

CTA: "Let's optimize and activate your villa"

Onboarding Tasks: Professional photos, description writing, listing setup (Airbnb, Booking.com, etc.), calendar sync, first guests scheduled

Follow-up: Weekly updates via WhatsApp + monthly dashboard access (booking rate, revenue, guest feedback)

Success Metric: First booking within 30 days of activation

Expected Monthly Funnel Volume (Goal: Month 6)

  • Website Visitors: 400-500/month (via Google Ads, organic search)
  • Leads Generated: 40-50 (8-12% conversion)
  • Calls Booked: 20-25 (50% conversion)
  • Visits Completed: 14-18 (70% conversion)
  • Contracts Signed: 8-11 (60% conversion)

  • Partnership pipeline: new agent relationships, co-marketing opportunities
  • Referral program results: any referrals yet? Incentive working?
  • Next month budget adjustment based on ROI

Quarterly Business Review (End of Q2 - June):

  • 3-month results vs. goals: 3 villas signed (on track or ahead?)
  • Revenue projection for year-end (20+ villas by December)
  • Customer satisfaction: guest ratings, owner feedback, retention rate
  • Team capacity: need to hire additional staff/photographers/coordinator?
  • Market feedback: any competitor moves? Pricing adjustments needed?
  • Strategy pivot: what's working exceptionally well? Allocate more budget. What's underperforming? Kill or improve it.
